## Source reliability notes

- **VAT - Ghana Revenue Authority** (https://gra.gov.gh/domestic-tax/tax-types/vat/) - Fetched through the web-fetch tool, which returns a model-written digest of the page rather than raw HTML.
- **Notice to all VAT registered taxpayers** (https://gra.gov.gh/news/portfolio/notice-to-all-vat-registered-taxpayers/) - The page shows no publication date. Fetched through the web-fetch tool (digest).
- **Withholding Tax - Ghana Revenue Authority** (https://gra.gov.gh/portfolio/withholding-tax/) - Fetched through the web-fetch tool (digest). The page wins where other sources disagree.
- **VAT Withholding - Ghana Revenue Authority** (https://gra.gov.gh/domestic-tax/tax-types/vat-withholding/) - Page still cites Act 870; the 2026 Guidelines (section 12.1) restate the 7% rule under Act 1151.
- **Practice Note on Withholding of Tax under the Income Tax Act, 2015 (Act 896), DT/2016/001, issued 6 October 2016** (https://gra.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Practice-Note-on-Withholding-of-Tax.pdf) - Downloaded and text-extracted with pdftotext. Issued in 2016, so rates in it are not used.
- **Withholding Tax Table - Residency, Tax Codes, Rate and Description (2015)** (https://gra.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/WHT-TAX-CODES_DESCRIPTION-AND-RATES_2015.pdf) - Older than the web page. Disagrees with the page on: services to an individual (15% here, 7.5% on the page) and resident petroleum subcontractors (15% here, 7.5% on the page). The page wins; neither row is modelled separately (individual services share the 7.5% services tax).
- **Value Added Tax Act, 2025 (Act 1151)** (https://gra.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/VALUE-ADDED-TAX-ACT-2025-ACT-1151.pdf) - Image-only PDF (87 pages): read through OCR, which is imperfect. Section numbers cited are as OCR-read.
- **VAT Administrative Guidelines for the Value Added Tax Act, 2025 (Act 1151), GRA/AG/25/002, 31 December 2025** (https://gra.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/VAT-Guidelines-for-VAT-ACT-1151.pdf) - Image-only PDF (22 pages): read through OCR, which is imperfect.
- **DT 110 Withholding Tax Return (v1.2, 2020)** (https://gra.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/withholding_tax_return_form_v1_2_2020-.xlsx) - Spreadsheet strings were read; formulas/validation lists were not analysed.
- **VAT on Imports and Exports - Ghana Revenue Authority** (https://gra.gov.gh/domestic-tax/tax-types/vat-on-imports-and-exports/) - Cites Act 1151 ss.35-37 and 50 for exports but does not state their content.
- **Income Tax Act, 2015 (Act 896)** (https://gra.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/INCOME-TAX-ACT-2015-ACT-896.pdf) - Could not be opened. Downloaded (HTTP 200) but not readable as text.
- **ISO 3166-2:GH - Online Browsing Platform** (https://www.iso.org/obp/ui/#iso:code:3166:GH) - Could not be opened. Not verified against the ISO platform.
- **ISO 3166-2:GH** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166-2:GH) - Secondary cross-check only. Agrees with the code list given in the build brief.
- **Accounting localization (Odoo 20.0 developer documentation)** (https://www.odoo.com/documentation/latest/developer/howtos/accounting_localization.html) - The fetch tool returned only the table of contents (the body was truncated), so the Odoo 20.0 source code below was used as the real reference.
- **Ghana - Corporate - Withholding taxes** (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ghana/corporate/withholding-taxes) - Agrees with the GRA page for every rate modelled. Disagrees on unprocessed precious minerals (1.5% vs GRA 3%) and lists extra rows (realisation of assets) absent from the GRA page; neither is modelled.
- **Ghana - Corporate - Other taxes** (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ghana/corporate/other-taxes) - Does not mention the threshold, flat rate scheme or COVID-19 levy, so nothing was taken from it.

## Where sources disagree

- **Services to an individual (resident)**: GRA WHT tax-code table 2015 says 15% (WRESSOSIOA); the current GRA withholding tax page says 7.5%. The GRA page is used (a single 7.5% services tax).
- **Resident petroleum subcontractors**: 15% (2015 table) vs 7.5% (GRA page). Not modelled.
- **Unprocessed precious minerals**: GRA page 3% vs PwC 1.5%. GRA wins; not modelled.
- **VAT withholding page** still cites Act 870; the 2026 Guidelines (s.12.1) restate the same 7% rule under Act 1151. Not modelled.
